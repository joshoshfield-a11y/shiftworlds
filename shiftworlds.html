Shiftworlds

A 2D platformer about two worlds in one place. Amber is warm and solid. Void is cold and strange. The course is split between them, the broken bridge only exists one world at a time, and the far beacon only exists in Void.

Chapters: I. Gather 12 shards  ·  II. Hold the crossing for 10 seconds  ·  III. Reach the far beacon in Void.

Controls: A / D move · Space jump (twice for double jump) · Shift dash · Q or F shift worlds · On touch, use the on-screen buttons.

Easier than the old 3D build: flat 2D view, wider platforms, small gaps, slower enemies, checkpoints, coyote time, and a hold timer that pauses instead of resetting.
